#0001/02 · Research · ColumbiaOngoing

Bayesian Recruitment Curves

Movement Recovery Laboratory · research for academic credit · Sep 2026 – now

Bayesian modeling of non-sigmoidal neural recruitment curves. When a nerve pathway is stimulated harder and harder, the muscle's response, the motor-evoked potential (MEP), grows along a "recruitment curve." Models usually assume an S shape, but real data often doesn't follow one. The goal is hierarchical Bayesian models that fit more flexible curves and show how much confidence each estimate deserves.

  • Extract MEPs from stimulation data and measure their size using the lab's Python pipelines
  • Build recruitment-curve models in the lab's hbMEP framework
  • Next: new curve functions, parameter uncertainty, and evaluating models on how well they predict

#0002/02 · Quant finance · ColumbiaIn progress

Replicating X-Trend

IEOR 4576 Data-Driven Models in Finance · team of 3 · Fall 2026

Few-shot learning for trend-following under regime change. Classic momentum strategies tend to break down when markets shift abruptly. X-Trend (Wood, Kessler, Roberts & Zohren, 2024) is a deep learning model that adapts by attending to similar past market regimes, which it finds using Gaussian-process change-point detection.

  • Replicating on 50 liquid futures (commodities, equity indices, rates, FX) from WRDS, 1990–2026
  • Extending the test to a new 2024–2026 out-of-sample period
  • Testing whether a regime-aware model beats buy-and-hold, time-series momentum, MACD, and a neural baseline without context on Sharpe ratio, especially in turbulent periods

Currently at the proposal stage. I'll post results here as they come in.

South African National Teams champion, age 16 (2018)

I started at age nine at the Links Bridge Club in Johannesburg, coached by Neville Eber. My father, Alon, has also played for South Africa.

I've since played for three countries. I played for England and the USA in World Youth events, and for South Africa in the Open series of the world championships, most recently in Herning, Denmark in 2025.

At its core, bridge is a game of probability and inference. A successful partnership has to communicate information based on the inferences each player picks up over the course of a hand.

Featured hand · Lyon 2017IBPA shortlist
5th World Youth Open Championships · Junior Teams

A double squeeze at 16

In the first round of the Junior Teams in Lyon, I was declaring 4♥ against a team from Finland. After drawing trumps, I reached a four-card ending in which neither opponent could keep all their guards. A double squeeze turned dummy's last ♦4 into the tenth trick.

The hand was worth 8 IMPs in a 37–0 win. It was written up in the championship's daily bulletin as "Fast learner" and later shortlisted for the IBPA's Richard Freeman Junior Deal of the Year.
